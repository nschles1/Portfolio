Scaling Digital Customer Acquisition

Owning the primary digital engine for acquiring new Card Members and driving profitable growth at scale for American Express.

The Context

As Product Lead for the U.S. Consumer acquisition website, I managed a platform serving over 1M monthly unique visitors. This was the central hub for acquiring new customers and increasing spend among existing members within a highly regulated financial environment.

Core Vision Pillars

  • Platform Redesign: Reimagining the card shopping experience with modern UX across 19 core products.
  • Targeted Infrastructure: Enabling predictive targeting and pre-approved offers to improve ROI.

Lessons Learned

In highly regulated environments, prioritization is everything. Understanding each stakeholder’s requirements—from Legal to Engineering—and aligning them to a clear strategy was critical to delivering impact without slowing execution.

Post-launch, we moved from "delivery mode" to "optimization mode," focusing on site performance, personalization refinement, and organic content strategy. The result was a platform built not just to launch—but to evolve.
