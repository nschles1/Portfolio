Context

As Product Lead for the American Express U.S. Consumer acquisition website, I owned the primary digital engine for acquiring new Card Members and driving profitable growth at scale.

The platform served over 1M monthly unique visitors and played a central role in acquiring new customers, increasing spend among existing Card Members, and supporting long-term organic growth — all within a highly regulated financial services environment.

Unified Responsive Platform Framework

The Objective

Platform Redesign

Reimagine the card shopping experience with modern UX and a unified responsive platform for 19 core Card products.

Targeted Infrastructure

Enable predictive targeting and pre-approved offers to support upsell and cross-sell opportunities through personalized flows.

Efficiency & ROI

Improve efficiency and ROI across digital acquisition channels through optimized conversion paths.

Personalization

Tailor the experience for existing Card Members to improve product fit and increase long-term spend.

My Role

As Product, Marketing, and Program Lead, I owned the strategy and end-to-end execution, balancing speed, scale, brand consistency, and regulatory rigor.

  • Defined the acquisition strategy and customer experience vision.
  • Led the full site redesign — from UX and content strategy through delivery.
  • Managed agency partners and all workstreams from concept to launch.
  • Drove cross-functional alignment across Legal, Compliance, Operations, and Engineering.
  • Partnered with 19 Card product owners to gather requirements and develop content.
  • Led offer development, testing, and optimization to improve conversion.

EXECUTION PRINCIPLES

Customer-first personalization

Help customers find the right card for their lifestyle and needs.

Platform simplification

Move from fragmented experiences to a unified responsive platform.

Compliance by design

Embed legal and regulatory requirements into workflows early.

Test-and-learn mindset

Launch, measure, and continuously optimize for performance.

Impact & Results

  • Generated **$3.5B** in annual revenue.
  • Acquired **700K** new accounts annually.
  • Supported over **1M** monthly unique visitors.
  • Delivered an **11% YoY increase** in new account acquisition following platform optimization.
  • Established scalable acquisition and personalization infrastructure for ongoing growth.

Lessons Learned

"In highly regulated, multi-stakeholder environments, prioritization is everything. Understanding each stakeholder’s requirements — and aligning them to a clear overarching strategy — was critical to delivering impact without slowing execution."

Post-launch, sustained growth required disciplined iteration. We established a structured optimization roadmap focused on site performance, personalization refinement, and organic content strategy.
