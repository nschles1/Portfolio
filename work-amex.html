Scaling Digital Customer Acquisition

Owning the primary digital engine for acquiring new Card Members and driving growth at scale.

Context

As Product Lead for the American Express U.S. Consumer acquisition website, I owned the primary digital engine for acquiring new Card Members and driving profitable growth at scale. The platform served over 1M monthly unique visitors and played a central role in acquiring new customers and supporting long-term organic growth—all within a highly regulated financial services environment.

The Vision

1. Platform Redesign

Reimagining the card shopping experience with modern UX and a unified responsive platform. We clearly articulated benefits across 19 core Card products and personalized the experience for existing members to improve product fit.

Unified Responsive Platform Framework

2. Targeted Marketing Infrastructure

Building predictive targeting and pre-approved offer capabilities to support upsell and cross-sell opportunities. This infrastructure improved efficiency and ROI across all digital acquisition channels.

Lessons Learned

In highly regulated, multi-stakeholder environments, prioritization is everything. Understanding each stakeholder’s requirements—and aligning them to a clear overarching strategy—was critical to delivering impact without slowing execution.

Post-launch, sustained growth required disciplined iteration. We established a structured optimization roadmap focused on site performance, personalization refinement, and organic growth through SEO and content strategy. The result was a platform built not just to launch—but to evolve.
