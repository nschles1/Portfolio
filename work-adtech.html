The Problem

Efficiency at the speed of light.

In the world of Real-Time Bidding (RTB), a millisecond of delay is a lost dollar. Our platform was struggling to maintain consistent performance during peak traffic spikes, leading to "bid-dropping" and lost revenue for our media buyers.

The Strategy

Edge Infrastructure

Moved core bidding logic to the edge, reducing round-trip time between SSPs and our engine.

Algorithmic Pruning

Introduced predictive modeling to filter out low-probability auctions before they hit the bidder.
