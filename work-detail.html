The Challenge

Legacy systems in a real-time world.

The existing architecture was built on a decade-old batch processing model. As transaction volume grew, settlement delays increased to 4+ hours, causing significant customer friction and regulatory risk. We needed to transition to a real-time, event-driven ledger without a single second of downtime.

The Strategy

Phased Migration

Implemented a "Strangler Fig" pattern to slowly move services to the new ledger, ensuring 0% data loss during transition.

Stakeholder Alignment

Managed cross-functional requirements between Engineering, Legal, and Compliance across three global regions.

The Outcome

"This re-architecture didn't just solve a technical debt—it unlocked a 22% increase in customer lifetime value by enabling instant payouts."
