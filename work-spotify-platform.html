A Vision Grounded in Customer Needs

I developed a clear vision and strategy that was grounded in customer needs. By partnering with the insights team, we developed a deep understanding of customer personas and the specific information they required to make a decision to advertise on Spotify.

Context

Spotify’s advertising business was scaling rapidly, but audio advertising was still an emerging channel for marketers. We needed a centralized platform that could both educate advertisers and drive measurable acquisition.

The strategy evolved from a primarily enterprise sales tool into a bifurcated experience serving both SMB and enterprise segments while maintaining an aggressive timeline.

My Role

I was brought in as the Program Manager Lead to drive the development and delivery of the project. My goal was to ensure we met business goals while creating a customer-focused experience that showcased the benefits of audio advertising.

I was responsible for driving cross-functional alignment across Product, Design, Marketing, Insights, Editorial Content, Brand, and Legal to meet an ambitious launch timeline.

Lessons Learned

"In high-growth environments, the ability to adapt post-launch is as important as the launch itself."

Post-launch analysis revealed opportunities to improve information hierarchy. Because we had built disciplined operating rhythms, we were able to quickly:

  • Partner with insights to gather user behavior data
  • Synthesize qualitative advertiser feedback
  • Prioritize structured optimizations for navigation and format visualization
