Context

Spotify’s advertising business was scaling rapidly, but audio advertising remained an emerging channel. We needed a platform that could simultaneously educate the market and drive measurable acquisition.

The strategy evolved from a primarily enterprise sales tool into a bifurcated experience: a self-serve entry point for SMBs and a high-touch funnel for enterprise brands.

My Role

I was brought in to operationalize the initiative from the ground up, establishing the execution framework and leading end-to-end delivery.

  • • Defined team structure & ownership models
  • • Built roadmap aligned to aggressive launch goals
  • • Instituted cross-functional planning cadences
  • • Led leadership reviews & priority trade-offs

Execution Principles

Clarity over Complexity

Simplifying audio education without losing the value proposition.

Dual-Path Architecture

Distinct, optimized journeys for SMB self-serve and Enterprise sales.

Lessons Learned

"In high-growth environments, the ability to adapt post-launch is as important as the launch itself."

Because we built disciplined operating rhythms, we were able to quickly gather user insights, synthesize feedback, and prioritize structured optimizations immediately following the launch.
