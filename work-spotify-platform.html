Launching the Spotify Advertising Marketing Platform

Establishing the foundational brand and conversion funnel for Spotify's advertising business.

Role

Program Management Lead

Scope

Execution & Strategy

Impact

~5% Sign-up Uplift

Sector

AdTech / Media

Context

Spotify’s advertising business was scaling rapidly, but audio advertising was still an emerging channel for marketers. We needed a centralized platform that could both educate advertisers and drive measurable acquisition.

A Vision Grounded in Customer Needs

I developed a clear vision and strategy that was grounded in customer needs. By partnering with the insights team, we developed a deep understanding of customer personas and the specific information they required to make a decision to advertise on Spotify.

My Role

I was brought in as the Program Manager Lead to drive the development and delivery of the project. My goal was to ensure we met business goals while creating a customer-focused experience that showcased the benefits of audio advertising.

Key Responsibilities

As Program Lead, I was responsible for end-to-end delivery:

  • Established the project’s operating model — defining core team structure, stakeholder map, roles & responsibilities, roadmap, and delivery milestones.
  • Created structured planning cadences and cross-functional forums to drive transparency and remove blockers.
  • Led alignment across Product, Design, Marketing, Insights, Editorial, Brand, Legal, and external agency partners.
  • Partnered with Insights to ground the website strategy in advertiser personas and decision-making needs.
  • Managed leadership reviews, approvals, and executive communications.
  • Helped shape the vision: positioning the site as both an educational platform for audio advertising and a performance-driven funnel for Ads growth.
  • Worked closely with Agency content team to develop the Unique value proportions and build out the educational content highlighting the benefit of Audio advertising.

Impact & Results

  • → Established the foundational marketing and acquisition framework for Spotify Advertising.
  • → Drove an immediate ~5% uplift in self-serve platform sign-ups.
  • → Increased overall site traffic and advertiser engagement.
  • → Created a scalable structure for ongoing iteration and optimization.

Lessons Learned

"In high-growth environments, the ability to adapt post-launch is as important as the launch itself."

Post-launch, we implemented a continuous improvement model — aggregating user feedback, analyzing behavioral data, and developing a structured optimization roadmap to improve conversion and clarity.
