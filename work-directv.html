Case Study 03

DIRECTV: Repositioning for the Streaming Era.

Driving customer acquisition and margin growth by translating technical streaming capabilities into a modern brand promise.

Cross-Funnel Strategy Framework

New Subscribers

220K+ / Qtr

Awareness Lift

35%

Context

DIRECTV was navigating slower subscriber growth amid intense competition from streaming-first platforms. While associated with satellite TV, higher margins lay in streaming products. The challenge: reposition the brand and drive demand toward streaming while delivering near-term results.

Strategic Approach

  • • Product-Led Positioning: Translated enhancements like universal search and watchlists into customer-centric value propositions.
  • • Operational Excellence: Streamlined campaign briefing, aligning Product, GTM, and Creative teams to reduce execution friction.
