The Challenge

DIRECTV was navigating slower subscriber growth amid intense competition from streaming-first platforms. While associated with satellite TV, higher margins lay in streaming products. The challenge: reposition the brand and drive demand toward streaming while delivering near-term acquisition results.

The Strategy

Product-Led Positioning

Translated technical streaming enhancements—like universal search and watchlists—into customer-centric value propositions that focused on "Less time browsing, more time watching."

Operational Excellence

Streamlined campaign briefing, aligning Product, GTM, and Creative teams to reduce execution friction and ensure consistent messaging across all acquisition funnels.

The Impact

"By bridging the gap between product capability and market perception, we didn't just drive numbers—we shifted the brand story. Operationalizing the 'Value Proposition Bible' ensured that every touchpoint, from social ads to the landing page, spoke with one high-converting voice."
