Objective

The challenge was twofold: reposition the brand to compete with streaming-first platforms and drive demand toward higher-margin streaming offerings while delivering near-term acquisition results.

Approach

Product-Led Positioning

Partnered with Product to translate streaming enhancements—unified live TV, universal search, and watchlists—into customer-centric value propositions.

Scalable Foundations

Created a centralized Value Proposition “Bible,” codifying core RTBs and claims by audience to enable consistent activation across GTM teams.

Operational Excellence

Streamlined the campaign briefing process, aligning stakeholders across Product, Creative, and Media to reduce friction and accelerate speed to market.

Lessons Learned

"Operational rigor scales impact: Streamlined processes didn’t just improve speed—they enabled better creative, tighter alignment, and more consistent results."
