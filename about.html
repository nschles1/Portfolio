My Approach

"I believe great program leadership is about creating clarity and accountability, and building trust across teams so people can move fast together."

When priorities, trade-offs, and success metrics are explicit, teams make better decisions. My job is to create clear strategies that drive both business and customer goals, align people around the work, reinforce ownership, and ensure teams understand the impact of what they're building.

The principle I keep coming back to: create meaningful products that genuinely delight consumers, with a team that finds real satisfaction in the everyday challenge of bringing those products to life. After 15+ years, I've learned that the teams who care deeply about both the product and the people building it are the ones who create something truly worth using.

I'm also actively learning and integrating AI tools into how I work — from streamlining planning workflows to improving how I synthesize insights and communicate strategy, to building this portfolio. In a field where operational efficiency is a competitive advantage, I think about AI as a force multiplier for the work, not a replacement for judgment.
