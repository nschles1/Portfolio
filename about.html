My Approach

"I believe great program leadership is about creating clarity and accountability, and building trust across teams so people can move fast together."

When priorities, trade-offs, and success metrics are explicit, teams make better decisions. My job is to create clear strategies that drive both business and customer goals, align people around the work, reinforce ownership, and ensure teams understand the impact of what they’re building.

The Journey

I started my career at the intersection of marketing and technology, always drawn to how complex systems can be streamlined for better outcomes. Over the past 15 years, I've had the privilege of working with global brands to navigate massive industry shifts—from the rise of mobile banking to the explosion of podcasting and the transition of linear TV to streaming.

Today, my focus is on helping organizations build the operational muscle needed to scale their most ambitious ideas. Whether it's a 0→1 launch or optimizing a $1B+ portfolio, I find the most joy in the "messy middle"—where strategy meets reality.
